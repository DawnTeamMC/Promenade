The **capybara** is a passive animal.
This large rodent is native to South America in real life, and it is the largest rodent of our world.
They are known for their social behavior and humorous laziness.

## Spawning

Capybaras can be found in rivers and swamps biomes in groups.

<Callout>
    You can tweak the frequency of the animals in the [mod's configuration file]($configuration).
</Callout>

## Variants

Baby capybaras have larger eyes and unique sounds.

When a capybara spawns, it has a 2% chance to be albino.

## Behavior

This lazy rodent tends to **sleep** during the day. It also sleeps at night, and when it does, it won't wake up until the sun rises again.

<Asset width={1024} location="promenade:entity/sleeping_capybara" title="Sleeping Capybara"/>

Adult capybaras also occasionally **farts**.

Capybaras are great **swimmers**. From time to time, they walk to nearby water to go for a swim, before heading back to land.
While swimming, they sometimes **dive** underwater for a few seconds, and come back up to the surface to breathe.

The capybara eats **carrots** and **melon slices**.
